STUDENT ENROLLMENT GUIDE

Enrollment Guidelines

Your guide to a simple, organized, and convenient enrollment experience. Follow these steps to complete your enrollment through EnrollEase at Taal High School.

Welcome to EnrollEase!

Before starting your enrollment, please read and follow the guidelines below. Providing accurate information and completing each step carefully will help ensure that your enrollment details are properly submitted.

How to Enroll

Follow these ten steps to complete the process.

01

Access the Enrollment System

Open the official EnrollEase website using a device with an internet connection. Read the instructions before proceeding.

02

Select Your Grade Level

Choose the appropriate grade level. Junior High School students may select Grades 7–10, while Senior High School students may select Grades 11–12.

03

Provide Accurate Information

Enter complete, correct, and updated personal details, including your LRN when applicable, address, and parent or guardian information.

04

Complete the Enrollment Form

Fill in all required fields and answer applicable questions. Senior High School students must select the appropriate semester, track, and strand or cluster when required.

05

Review Your Information

Check every entry before submitting the form. Correct any mistakes and make sure all required information is complete.

06

Submit Your Enrollment

Click the enrollment button after completing the form. Wait for the system's response and check whether your submission was successful.

07

Keep Your Registration ID

Save your assigned Registration ID and other enrollment details. Keep them for future reference and enrollment record retrieval.

08

Download Your Enrollment Documents

Follow the available instructions to access or download your enrollment documents. Save a copy for your personal records.

09

Protect Your Personal Information

Keep your Registration ID and personal information confidential. Do not share sensitive enrollment details with unauthorized individuals.

10

Ask for Assistance When Needed

Contact authorized school personnel if you experience technical problems, encounter incorrect records, or need help with the enrollment process.

!

Important Reminder

Always verify your information before submitting the enrollment form. Keep your enrollment records secure and follow the school's official enrollment procedures. Submitting this form does not necessarily mean that your enrollment has been officially approved.
